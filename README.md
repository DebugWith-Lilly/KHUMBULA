# ✦ KHUMBULA

### *Every Face. Every Story. Remembered.*

---

## ✦ Problem Statement

Memory loss can make everyday life challenging, affecting an individual's ability to recognise familiar faces, recall conversations, remember important information, and maintain independence.

Forgetting people, personal interactions, and everyday details can create feelings of uncertainty, frustration, and isolation. There is a need for accessible support that helps individuals reconnect with their memories and navigate daily interactions with greater confidence.

---

## 🌿 Our Solution — KHUMBULA

Developed by **Team CtrlAltDefeat**, KHUMBULA is a memory-support platform designed to assist individuals experiencing memory loss with recognising familiar people, recalling previous interactions, and keeping track of important daily reminders.

Through a connected mobile application and optional smart glasses integration, KHUMBULA creates a more accessible way to revisit personal memories and navigate everyday interactions.

The platform brings together three interconnected features:

- 🌿 **Face Recognition** — Helps users identify familiar people and access relevant information about them.
- 🕊️ **Conversation Recall** — Provides brief reminders of previous interactions to help users reconnect with conversations and personal connections.
- ✧ **Daily Reminders** — Helps users keep track of important tasks, events, and everyday activities.

---

## ✧ Our Mission

We envision a future where memory loss does not have to mean losing meaningful connections or independence.

KHUMBULA aims to support individuals in preserving their connections, revisiting meaningful interactions, and navigating everyday life with greater confidence — placing **dignity, independence, accessibility, and human connection** at the heart of the experience.
